# Artist booking record checks

Sources checked 9 November 2026. These checks support the agency's preparation and record-keeping. They do not certify event safety, interpret contract enforceability, calculate tax, issue visas or process payments.

| Rule | Trigger | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| HOLD-EXPIRED | Missing or elapsed hold deadline | Internal booking policy, not a statutory deadline |
| CONTRACT-MISSING | Confirmed show without a signed reference, date or recording operator | Internal evidence policy |
| BOOKING-CLASH | Artist booked for overlapping active time intervals | Internal scheduling policy |
| DEPOSIT-OVERDUE | Unpaid agreed deposit past its recorded due date | The recorded booking agreement, not a standard legal term |
| ADVANCE-OVERDUE | Preparation task incomplete after its due date | Internal preparation policy |
| SAFETY-REVIEW | Confirmed show within fourteen days has no completed safety task | Internal reminder supporting coordination of event risks |
| TRAVEL-LATE | Recorded journey arrives after the show begins | Internal logistics check; travel feasibility and buffer time need human review |
| PRIVACY-REVIEW | Artist or promoter retention review missing or elapsed | NZ Privacy Act principle 9 plus the agency's chosen review date |

[WorkSafe: Managing risks at events](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/topic-and-industry/event-management/managing-risks-at-events/) describes the responsibility to manage risks to workers and other people. A booking agency must understand its own role and coordinate with the promoter and venue. The fourteen-day reminder is our demo policy, not a deadline imposed by WorkSafe. The record requires a responsible operator, review date and evidence reference. The program cannot inspect the evidence or establish that a control works.

[New Zealand Privacy Commissioner: Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) limits retention to the period needed for a lawful purpose. Choose review dates from that purpose and applicable obligations. The system flags a review and never deletes records automatically. Australian operations need their own applicable privacy and event obligations checked during setup.

Itinerary drafts require a confirmed, future or ongoing show, recorded contract evidence, a completed safety item, no open preparation tasks, no booking clashes, no late arrivals and the agreed deposit fully recorded. This is an internal completeness gate. A show without recorded travel does not prove transport is arranged. Confirm transport needs and actual safety arrangements with the responsible people.

Generic documents show incomplete records deliberately so a coordinator can review them. Only the draft-itinerary command applies the release gate. All output remains an internal draft. No command sends it or makes a legal declaration.
