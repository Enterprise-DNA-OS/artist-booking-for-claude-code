# Why this desk has no front end

The agent operates the agency records through one CLI. Read-only HTML shows the week, deposits and preparation. Documents are snapshots you can review and print.

SystemOne also offers a mobile itinerary app, external advancing, electronic signatures, calendar connections and live flight details. This base does not provide mobile editing, offline synchronization, a drag-and-drop calendar, electronic signatures, flight feeds or message delivery. It does not replace a public artist discovery network. Dates, signed references and receipts come from the operator.

The useful unit here is the internal booking desk: records, clear checks, reviewed drafts and portable history. Enterprise DNA scopes mobile access, external forms and connections in a customer's version. The database belongs to the agency. The paid service is Omni by Enterprise DNA.
